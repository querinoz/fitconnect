# ASCEND challenges & missions

Missions: DAILY (move **or** recover), WEEKLY 20 km, MONTHLY 80 km, PERSONAL, plus coach-assigned.

Challenges: AVAILABLE → JOINED → ACTIVE → COMPLETED / EXPIRED.

Squad challenge `squad-fc-week` shows **contribution**, not a shame ranking. Recovery distribution is called out as protected.

Architecture is local-first; server-backed challenges are a later reconcile.
